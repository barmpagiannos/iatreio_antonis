Hello, I’m Dr Metaxas

A doctor who believes in enough time.

I grew up in a family where the kitchen table was the place for every important conversation. That’s still how I think about primary care: a welcoming place to ask honest questions, understand the full picture, and make a plan that feels doable.

After training at the University of Texas and completing my residency at St. David’s Medical Center, I founded this practice to offer the kind of care I want for my own family — rigorous, warm, and built around a lasting relationship.
